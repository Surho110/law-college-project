Our Mission & History

Founded on Integrity

Established in 1995, The Justice Academy has been a cornerstone of legal excellence. We believe in the rule of law as a tool for social change and justice. Our alumni serve in the highest courts and most prestigious firms globally.

Our Vision

To be a global leader in legal education and research.

Our Values

Justice, Ethics, Courage, and Intellectual Rigor.
